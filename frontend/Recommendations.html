Energy Recommendations

Smart insights to optimize your energy usage

menu_book Understanding Your Recommendations

circle Green — Great Time

Battery is above 80% and your system is generating well. Run heavy appliances like washing machines, geysers, and ovens now to use free solar/wind power.

circle Orange — Moderate

Battery is between 40–80%. Use appliances cautiously. Consider waiting for better generation or charge levels before running high-draw devices.

circle Red — Avoid Now

Battery is below 30% or no generation happening. Delay heavy appliances to avoid draining your battery or drawing expensive power from the grid.
