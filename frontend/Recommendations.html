Appliance Shift — Traffic Light

circle Green — Great Time

Battery above 80% and generating well. Run heavy appliances (washing machine, geyser, oven) now.

circle Orange — Moderate

Battery between 40–80%. Use appliances cautiously. Wait for better conditions if possible.

circle Red — Avoid Now

Battery below 30% or no generation. Delay heavy appliances to avoid grid draw.
