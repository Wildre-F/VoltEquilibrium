Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026 · This page is a placeholder and will be replaced with the full legal document before launch.

1. Information We Collect

We collect account registration data (name, email, password hash), device telemetry (inverter readings, battery state, location coordinates used for weather data), and usage analytics to improve the platform. No data is sold to third parties.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the VoltEquilibrium monitoring service — displaying live readings, calculating CO₂ savings, and generating analytics reports for your own devices.

3. Data Retention

Raw telemetry readings are retained for up to 12 months. Account data is retained until you request deletion. You may delete all your data at any time from the Profile page.

4. Security

Passwords are hashed using bcrypt. API keys are generated cryptographically. All communication between the client and server uses HTTPS in production. JWT tokens expire after 1 hour.

5. Contact

For privacy-related requests, use the Contact Support page accessible from any dashboard sidebar.
